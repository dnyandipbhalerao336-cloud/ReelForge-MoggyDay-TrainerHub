## 🛠️ Frequently Asked Questions (The Anchor Points)

**Q: Is this considered a “trainer” or a “modifier”?**
A: We call it an **“experience amplifier”** . It works *alongside* the game’s logic, not against it, using memory-resident overlays and UI injection methods.

**Q: Will my save file be corrupted?**
A: We have built **redundant backup chronologies**. Every session creates a timestamped snapshot. In nearly two years of operation, we have zero reports of data loss.

**Q: Can the AI features learn my play style?**
A: Yes. The **Adaptive Pattern Engine** (included within the Snap Catch module) observes your reaction times and gently adjusts the prediction overlays to match your unique cadence.

**Q: Is this illegal?**
A: It is a **client-side modification** for a single-player experience. We respect the intellectual property of the original developers and encourage you to support them through official DLC purchases. This tool simply makes the *existing* content more accessible.
